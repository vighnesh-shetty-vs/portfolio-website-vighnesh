Retail Bank Analytics: dbt Warehouse + Power BI

Real data from a Czech bank (1.06M transactions, 682 loans) modelled in dbt on DuckDB: 17 models, 55 tests including daily balance reconciliation and a no-leakage check, 10 SQL business questions, and a 3-page Power BI report (21 DAX measures, row-level security).

Finding: all 27 loans granted to clients who had already been overdrawn defaulted, vs 7.5% for everyone else. The bank had that information before lending.

Credit Card Default Scorecard

Bank-style points scorecard on 30,000 real card customers: monotonic WoE binning, IV selection, logistic regression scaled to points (PDO 20), validated with Gini, KS, calibration and PSI, plus a fairness check. 7 unit tests and CI.

Result: test Gini 0.51, KS 0.40. The cost-based cut-off approves 54% of customers at an 11% default rate vs 22% overall.

Card Fraud Detection Engine

XGBoost classifier on 284,807 European card transactions (0.17% fraud) with class weighting. Each blocked transaction is explained by its top SHAP feature, and a Streamlit dashboard replays held-out transactions next to their true labels.

Result: PR-AUC 0.86 on the full test set; at a 0.9 threshold it catches 82% of frauds with 90% precision.

Neobank Onboarding A/B Test

End-to-end experiment: SQL cleaning of messy event data (duplicates, double exposure, bots), pre-registered power analysis, sample-ratio check, guardrail metrics and Bonferroni-corrected segments. Synthetic data with a known true effect, so the method is checked in CI.
